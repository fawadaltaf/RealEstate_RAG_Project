1. ARCHIVED BROKERAGE COMMISSION & SERVICE FEES (v1.0)
- Residential & Commercial Sale Commission: Flat 2% charged exclusively to the Buyer (0% from the Seller).
- Rental / Leasing Commission: Full 1 month's rent charged exclusively to the Tenant (Landlord paid 0%).
- Legal Title & NOC Verification Fee: Flat PKR 15,000 non-refundable fee for all clients.

2. ARCHIVED TOKEN MONEY & DOWN PAYMENT RULES (v1.0)
- Minimum Token (Bayana) Amount: Flat PKR 200,000 regardless of property value, locking the property for 15 calendar days.
- Cash Acceptance Limit: Cash token payments were accepted up to PKR 1,500,000 under Policy v1.0.
- Security Deposit on Rentals: 6 months' security deposit was mandatory on all residential and commercial rentals.

3. ARCHIVED TOKEN REFUND & CANCELLATION TIMELINE (v1.0)
- Refund Timeline: Token refunds in case of seller default took 15 to 20 business days and were issued only via post-dated company cheques.
