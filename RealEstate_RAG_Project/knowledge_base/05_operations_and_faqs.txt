1. PROPERTY TRANSFER & SITE VISIT PROCEDURES
- Site Visit Booking Cut-Off: Site visit requests submitted before 2:00 PM PKT (Monday to Saturday) are scheduled the same afternoon with an assigned BQRE Field Advisor. Requests after 2:00 PM PKT are scheduled for the next working day.
- Typical Transfer & Mutation Timelines:
  * DHA / Bahria Town Society Transfer: 5 to 7 working days after NOC issuance.
  * KDA / SBCA Sub-Registrar Sale Deed Registration: 10 to 14 working days.
  * CDA Islamabad Sector Transfer: 7 to 10 working days.

2. DOCUMENT CHECKLIST FOR PROPERTY TRANSFER
- Sellers must submit: Original Allotment/Title Deed, Paid Utility Bills, Society NDC (No Demand Certificate), and valid CNIC/NICOP copy.
- Buyers must submit: Valid CNIC/NICOP copy, FBR Active Taxpayer List (ATL) Filer Certificate, and Pay Order for stamp duty/transfer fee.

3. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQs)
Q1: Can Overseas Pakistanis buy or transfer property through BQRE without traveling to Pakistan?
A: Yes! Overseas Pakistanis holding a valid NICOP or POC can execute a Special Power of Attorney (SPA) attested by the Pakistani Embassy/Consulate in their country of residence, or use NADRA Digital Power of Attorney for DHA and CDA transfers.

Q2: Do you offer discounts for investors buying multiple residential plots or studio apartments?
A: Yes. Investors purchasing 3 or more units or plots in a single transaction (such as BQRE Skyline Studio Apartments or DHA Phase 9 Prism Plots) qualify for a 25% discount on BQRE's buyer-side brokerage commission when booked through corporate@banoqabilrealestate.pk.

Q3: What is the difference in advance tax (Section 236K) for FBR Filers versus Non-Filers?
A: Under current FBR rules, Active FBR Filers pay 3% advance withholding tax on property purchase, whereas Non-Filers pay 10.5% advance tax. BQRE's legal desk verifies ATL status prior to Pay Order preparation.

Q4: Does BQRE assist with home financing or Islamic mortgage (Musharakah) approvals?
A: Yes. BQRE has pre-approved panel arrangements with Meezan Bank Easy Home and Bank Alfalah Islamic Housing Finance for ready-to-possess properties above PKR 15,000,000, financing up to 70% of the property valuation for 5 to 20 years.

Q5: Who pays for routine maintenance in rented properties managed by BQRE?
A: Minor routine repairs below PKR 10,000 (such as plumbing washers or electrical switches) are covered by the Tenant, while major structural, roof waterproofing, or main pipeline repairs above PKR 10,000 are borne by the Landlord.
