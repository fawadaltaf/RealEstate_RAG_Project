1. ABOUT   REAL ESTATE & BUILDERS
  Real Estate & Builders (BQRE) is a registered Pakistani real estate brokerage, property management, and residential development agency operating across Karachi, Lahore, and Islamabad. Founded to bring transparent documentation, verified title checks, and structured installment plans to buyers and tenants, BQRE serves residential homebuyers, commercial investors, overseas Pakistani clients, and corporate tenants.

2. CORE SERVICES
- Residential & Commercial Property Brokerage (Sale, Purchase, and Long-Term Leasing).
- Title Verification, NOC Due Diligence, and Sub-Registrar / Society File Transfer Assistance.
- Turnkey Rental Property Management (Tenant screening, rent collection, and maintenance supervision).
- Off-Plan Developer Project Bookings with flexible quarterly installment schedules.
- Property Valuation & Market Feasibility Advisory for corporate and retail investors.
